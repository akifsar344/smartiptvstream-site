Frequently asked questions

Does Smart IPTV Stream provide channels or content?

No. Smart IPTV Stream is a player only. You bring your own Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist from your provider; we don't sell, host or supply any channels or media.

Which devices are supported?

LG (webOS) and Samsung (Tizen) smart TVs, Android TV, and any modern web browser on phones, tablets and computers at this site.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay once for Lifetime Pro — there are no subscriptions or recurring fees. You also get a free 1-day trial to try everything first.

Can I use HTTP-only playlists in the browser?

Yes. Insecure HTTP streams are automatically proxied over HTTPS so they play safely in the browser without mixed-content errors.

Do my favourites and progress sync?

Yes. Your playlists, favourites, continue-watching and settings are tied to your account and follow you across devices.
